Important Notes

Learning Program

Skiing is organized as a guided group lesson led by experienced instructors, ensuring a fun, safe, and encouraging learning environment for all participants. Groups are split by age (adults and children learn separately) and by skiing level, so everyone progresses at their own pace.

Ski Resorts

Ski resorts may vary depending on participants' skiing abilities as well as snow and weather conditions.

Guests who do not ski are welcome to relax and enjoy the wellness facilities and swimming pool. Free time activities are not guided.

Getting There in Winter

Bohinjska Bistrica is a regular town, not a remote resort: shops and restaurants nearby, the train station is a 10 minute walk, and there is a large parking area at the apartments.
